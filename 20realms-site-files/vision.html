Vision

A VTT that feels alive.

20Realms combines top-down 3D play, optional 2D and immersive camera modes, world-building tools, reactive environments, survival systems, console availability, and flexible rules so groups can keep playing even when they cannot meet in person.

Design goal

More table, less friction.

The goal is not to replace the Dungeon Master. It is to give every group a shared space where maps, characters, dice, combat, exploration, and homebrew tools stay connected across mobile, PC, and console without taking the personality out of the game.

Roadmap

20Realms Game Development Roadmap

Phase 1 • Title

20Realms Game Development Roadmap

5e-compatible build plan with beta target in November 2026 and full launch target in Summer 2027.

Phase 2 • Vision and outcomes

Stable platform with KPI iteration

Build a stable digital tabletop RPG platform end-to-end, keep mechanics 5e-compatible with open-license content, and iterate from beta using KPI data.

Phase 3 • 5e compatibility guardrails

Core rules consistency

Preserve standard 5e math and action economy, consistently implement conditions and saves, and keep all rules content SRD/open-license/original only.

Phase 4 • Technical foundation

Architecture, permissions, and schemas

Finalize architecture for accounts, characters, campaigns, encounters, and maps, implement secure auth and audit controls, and define rules/content schemas.

Phase 5 • Character core

Character creator and sheet v1

Deliver class/species/background setup, derived stats, inventory hooks, and validation for HP, AC, initiative, saves, proficiency, and level scaling.

Phase 6 • Campaign and session core

Party flow and DM prep

Ship campaign creation, party management, lore and notes tools, reusable encounter templates, shared handouts, and stable session state handling.

Phase 7 • Combat and encounter engine

Turn-state and resolution logic

Implement initiative tracking, attack/damage/save resolution, conditions and concentration logic, rest refresh systems, and multiplayer-safe combat event logging.

Phase 8 • Spells and equipment

Rules content pipeline

Build spellcasting systems, equipment proficiency handling, and a rules QA pipeline for consistent and legal-safe SRD/open-license/original publishing.

Phase 9 • Maps and Creator Lab

Worldbuilding systems

Deliver terrain, elevation, walls, lighting, token layers, lore-linked world modules, and Creator Lab workflows with moderation and reporting controls.

Phase 10 • Multiplayer and safety

Community and moderation hardening

Ship real-time session sync resilience, community discovery, creator profiles, moderation tooling, abuse reporting, and privacy/security hardening.

Phase 11 • QA and launch ops

Closed beta and KPI patch cadence

Run structured beta onboarding, triage issues weekly, track acquisition/conversion/retention/revenue KPIs, and execute a multi-channel support playbook.

Phase 12 • Milestones

Release sequence

Complete core and QA baseline, launch beta in November 2026, stabilize after beta, and target full launch in Summer 2027 with ongoing live operations.

Phase 13 • Build order

First to last implementation path

Build architecture first, then character systems, campaign/session flow, combat mechanics, rules content, worldbuilding, multiplayer safety, and live operations.

Phase 14 • Dependencies and risks

Scope, legal, community, and platform controls

Maintain SRD/open-license/original-only content policy, phase-gate advanced scope, prepare moderation before scale, and keep KPI strategy adaptable by platform.
